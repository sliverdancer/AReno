# Publication feasibility assessment

Assessment date: `2026-08-02`

## Decision

`CONDITIONAL_OPPORTUNITY_NOT_SUBMISSION_READY`

There remains a credible paper opportunity because the current full-text audit
found no direct substitute for the complete prospective interaction: base
checkpoint reward resolution as moderator, AF/LF/AN/LN eligibility as the
treatment, strict multi-turn tool success as outcome, and both update-count and
trainable-token controls.

The evidence is currently question-level, not result-level. RIST-v1.1 did not
produce a valid cross-family qualification, and the first RIST-v2 revision
stopped at a CPU evaluator defect. There is no training interaction, seed-level
uncertainty, real-environment external validity, or confirmatory result.

## What would make it publishable

A defensible workshop or Findings-style diagnostic paper would require at
minimum a new protocol revision with prospectively transporting calibration,
two completed model families, and a small independently seeded factorial
pilot. A competitive main-conference paper additionally requires:

1. a stable, practically meaningful reward-resolution-by-topology interaction;
2. independent training seeds as the replication unit and prospective power;
3. step-matched and cumulative-trainable-token-matched controls;
4. CIGPO/Signal Reshaping and relevant turn-credit baselines;
5. at least one real multi-turn tool environment in addition to the synthetic
   causal instrument;
6. complete raw evidence, failure accounting, and cross-setting consistency.

## Kill conditions

Stop the paper route if model-conditional calibration does not transport to
fresh nonces, if the interaction is practically negligible with adequate
power, if required seed counts are infeasible, or if a direct substitute
appears before training.

The current D2 KILL does not lower belief in the scientific hypothesis; it does
show that protocol engineering must be made more reliable before scarce GPU
resources are rented again.
