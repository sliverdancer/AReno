# RIST-v2 literature position

Evidence date: `2026-08-01` full-text audit inherited from RIST-v1.1 R0.

The audit covered 557 retrieved records, 496 deduplicated titles, 150 retained
records, and full-text review of the mandatory and elevated threat set. No
single inspected work combined all five elements of the proposed contribution:
multi-turn tool learning, pre-treatment reward resolution as moderator,
manipulated supervision eligibility, moderator-by-supervision interaction, and
strict success/stability/sample efficiency.

The gap is narrow. CIGPO and AVSPO occupy reward/advantage collapse; Signal
Reshaping occupies all/last-adjacent failure masking; MatchTIR and ToolPRM
occupy turn credit and name/argument decomposition; TopoCurate and ProxMO
occupy resolution-aware task selection or gradient modulation. RIST-v2 is
defensible only as a prospective causal moderation study under token-mass
controls, not as a new mask, credit method, or reward-collapse discovery.

Primary sources and exact non-overlap are recorded in
`../rist_v1_1/stages/R0/FULL_TEXT_REVIEW.md` and
`../rist_v1_1/stages/R0/reviewer_threat_matrix.json`.

## Publication consequence

The question retains conditional novelty. Publication viability now depends on
measurement validity: model-conditional reward resolution must transport from
calibration nonces to fresh qualification nonces. Without that, the moderator
is post hoc and the project should stop before training. With transport plus a
seed-level factorial interaction, token-matched robustness, and a real tool
environment, the work can support a main-conference diagnostic contribution.
