## Interpretation

P1 establishes that:

1. terminal outcome broadcast is structurally unable to recover exact local
   turn signs in the controlled environment;
2. turns from one trajectory cannot be counted as independent calibration
   units;
3. a fixed-denominator gradient-mass loss yields a nested family compatible
   with conformal risk control; and
4. the guarantee is iteration-local and requires calibration/update blocks
   from the same frozen behavior-policy distribution.

The result is deliberately conservative. With too few calibration
trajectories, the valid action is full abstention. This is a data requirement,
not an implementation bug.

## Novelty boundary

The statistical theorem is a conditional application of conformal risk
control, not a new general conformal theorem. Main-conference viability
therefore depends on the agentic contribution surviving later gates:

- exact preservation of turn boundaries and signed token routing;
- density- and interaction-matched controls;
- empirical utility beyond ordinary threshold tuning;
- explicit cost of counterfactual audits; and
- iteration-local calibration under on-policy drift.

If the P2/P3 method is operationally indistinguishable from top-k thresholding,
the route must return `KILL_INCREMENTAL_ONLY`.
